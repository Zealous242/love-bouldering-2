About BoulderWiki

BoulderWiki is a community knowledge base created for people who want to learn about bouldering. Visitors can browse published articles, search for specific topics, and explore information by category.

Our content covers subjects such as climbing techniques, equipment, safety, training, grading systems, climbing holds, and bouldering terminology. Whether you are new to the sport or an experienced climber, BoulderWiki is designed to make useful information easy to find and understand.

Registered users can contribute by commenting on articles and suggesting improvements. Suggestions are reviewed by administrators before they can change published content, helping keep the information accurate, reliable, and useful for the whole community.

Our goal is to build an accessible, organised, and community-supported resource where climbers can learn, explore, and share their knowledge.
